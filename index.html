Working Papers and Preprints

  1. Maximal Stability of Random Order of Service in Redundancy Systems

    Haojie Yan (2026). Preprint.

    SSRN

    Summary

    Proves the conjectured strict Hall sufficiency for ROS under Poisson arrivals, with heterogeneous servers, arbitrary compatibility graphs, and arbitrarily correlated copies; extends it to general unit-mean service distributions. Controls duplicate work through truncation and martingale bounds, then combines a logarithmically weighted capacity comparison with a fluid–Lyapunov argument. Full-state contraction yields positive Harris recurrence and an affine expected emptying-time bound without higher-moment assumptions.

  2. Integrating Market Selection, Inventory Control, and Pricing Decisions

    Xin Chen, Shuai Li, Weijun Xie, and Haojie Yan (2025).
    INFORMS Journal on Optimization, minor revision.

    SSRN

    Summary

    Develops exact methods for mixed-integer nonlinear optimization of market selection, inventory control, and pricing. For decoupled pricing, shortest-path dynamic programming yields optimal dual solutions and closed-form Benders cutting planes. For coupled pricing with piecewise-linear concave ordering costs, combines network-flow integrality and perspective reformulations to characterize the subproblem's closed convex hull and strengthen the full model.

  3. On the KL-Divergence-based Robust Satisficing Model

    Haojie Yan, Minglong Zhou, and Jiayi Guo (2024). Preprint.

    arXiv

    Summary

    Derives exponential-moment reformulations for general loss functions, empirical tail bounds, and finite-sample risk guarantees with Laplace smoothing on finite support. Develops normalized stochastic feasibility tests with unbiased gradients for the basic model, coupled with bisection in the robustness parameter. Uses the KL chain rule to separate group and conditional distribution shifts in a hierarchical model, exploiting convexity for nested bisection and golden-section search.
